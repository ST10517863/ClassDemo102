Organisation's history

Upon research we have noticed that a lot of students who struggle with their academics often don't have access to tutors as they are expensive

Vision

To ensure that all students have access to quality study support regardless of their financial background and learn using study techniques that are suitable for them

Mission

Connect tutors SA's mission is to provide free and accessible tutoring by connecting students to tutors based on their academic needs and preffered learning techniques
